📋 Tax & Compliance
Philippine VAT Law (TRAIN Law — RA 10963)
Standard rate: 12%. VAT-registered businesses must collect and remit VAT monthly/quarterly to the BIR. Senior Citizens (RA 9994) and PWD (RA 10754) are entitled to a 20% discount + VAT exemption on qualifying purchases. Businesses with annual gross sales ≤ ₱3,000,000 may opt for the 3% Percentage Tax instead of VAT.
