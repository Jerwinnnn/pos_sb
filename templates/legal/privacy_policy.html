This system is an internal Point-of-Sale tool used only by authorised staff of {{ app_name }}. It is not a public-facing website. This policy explains what data is processed, how it is stored, and your rights as a user of this system.

1. Who We Are

{{ app_name }} ("we", "the Store") operates this POS system and is responsible for the personal data processed through it. For any data privacy concerns, contact the system administrator.

2. What Data We Collect

Staff Accounts

  • Full name and username
  • Hashed password (the original password is never stored or readable)
  • Role (cashier or administrator)
  • Last login timestamp

Shift Records

  • Clock-in and clock-out times
  • Opening and closing cash amounts entered by the staff member
  • Optional shift notes

Transactions

  • Order items, quantities, prices, and totals
  • Payment method (e.g. Cash, Card, or digital payment)
  • Any payment reference number entered by the cashier
  • Name of the staff member who processed the sale
  • Transaction timestamp

What We Do Not Collect

This system does not collect customer names, customer contact details, biometric data, government-issued IDs, or any other personal information from customers. Receipts show only the order total and payment method.

3. How We Use the Data

  • Authenticating staff logins and managing system access
  • Recording shift attendance and cash reconciliation
  • Generating sales reports and end-of-day summaries
  • Maintaining a transaction audit trail for accountability

4. Third-Party Services

This system loads fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com). This causes your browser to make a request to Google's servers, which may log your IP address. No other third-party tracking, analytics, or advertising services are used. For details, see our Cookie Policy.

5. Data Storage and Security

All data is stored in a local database on the store's server. The system is not connected to external databases or cloud storage. Access is protected by username/password authentication with role-based access control.

6. Data Retention

Transaction records and shift logs are kept indefinitely for accounting and audit purposes. Staff accounts can be deactivated or deleted by an administrator at any time. Deactivated accounts retain their historical records for audit purposes but cannot log in.

7. Your Rights

As a staff member using this system, you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of your personal data held in this system
  • Rectification — request correction of inaccurate data
  • Erasure — request deletion of your account when it is no longer needed
  • Object — raise concerns about how your data is being processed

To exercise these rights, contact the system administrator.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in the system. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated accordingly. Continued use of the system after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
