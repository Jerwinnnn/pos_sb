This system uses a small number of cookies and makes limited third-party requests. No advertising or tracking cookies are used.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored in your browser. They are used to keep you logged in and remember lightweight preferences during your session.

2. Cookies We Use

Session Cookie (Essential)

When you sign in, the system sets a session cookie that keeps you authenticated while the browser is open. This cookie is essential for the system to function — without it, you cannot stay logged in. It is a first-party, session-only cookie that is deleted when you close the browser or sign out. No consent is required for essential session cookies.

Name Purpose Type Duration
session Keeps you logged in Essential / First-party Session (cleared on close or sign-out)
cookie_consent Remembers that you acknowledged this notice Functional / First-party Session (stored in server-side session)

3. Third-Party Requests

Google Fonts

This system loads fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.com). When your browser loads a page, it makes a request to Google's servers to download the font files. Google may log your IP address as part of this request. This is a one-time request per session for display purposes only. No advertising profile is created from it. You can review Google's privacy practices at policies.google.com/privacy.

Chart.js (jsDelivr CDN)

The sales reports page loads Chart.js from the jsDelivr CDN (cdn.jsdelivr.net). This is a standard content-delivery network request with no user tracking involved. jsDelivr's privacy policy is available at jsdelivr.com.

4. No Tracking or Analytics

This system does not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any other analytics, advertising, or behavioural tracking tools. No data about your activity within this system is sent to third parties beyond what is described in Section 3.

5. No Third-Party Embeds

This system does not embed content from social media platforms, payment gateways, or advertising networks. All third-party resource requests are limited to the font and charting libraries described above.

6. Managing Cookies

You can view, block, or delete cookies through your browser's settings at any time. Clearing the session cookie will sign you out. All major browsers (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Safari) provide cookie management options in their Settings or Privacy menus.
